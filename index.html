MARSWALK

An interplanetary survival guide for the first people to walk on Mars. Plan a safe, science-rich Marswalk on real Jezero Crater terrain, then feel the stakes in a field-operations simulation.

REAL NASA / USGS DATA

Field Systems planner

Offline tactical traverse planner for the Jezero western delta.

  • Six integrated layers: CTX imagery, 20 m DEM and slope, THEMIS, CRISM, SIM 3464 geology
  • Optimal multi-stop science traverses that avoid slopes above 15°
  • Instant re-routing around reported obstructions
  • Visor HUD, 3D astronaut view, EVA time budget
Open planner → SIMULATION GAME

Field Operations

Choose a landing cell on the global Mars map, descend, and explore on foot.

  • Global NASA Mars Trek basemap with selectable landing grid
  • Third- and first-person EVA with oxygen and battery management
  • Fictional telemetry, labelled as simulated
  • Desktop keyboard/mouse and touch controls
Play →
The planner's measurements come from NASA Mars Trek CTX imagery, the USGS Mars 2020 CTX DEM, USGS SIM 3464, MRO CRISM, THEMIS-derived thermal inertia and Perseverance MEDA, all cited in the app. Times, reserves and slope limits are user assumptions. Neither app is flight software or a safety certification.
